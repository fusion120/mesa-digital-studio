05 / Good to know

Questions?
Answered.

What do I need to get started?

A short description of your business, preferred colors, logo if you have one, photos, services, and the contact or booking link you'd like customers to use.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your agreed website deliverables and business content are yours. Hosting, domain registration, and any third-party services should remain in accounts you control.

Does the ad package include money spent on ads?

No. Our fees cover creative work and any specifically listed setup services. Your media budget is separate and paid directly through your own advertising account.

How long will a project take?

Delivery depends on the package, requested functionality, and how quickly content and feedback arrive. We'll agree on a realistic timeline before work begins.

Can you guarantee more customers?

No agency can honestly guarantee a specific number of customers from a website or ad. We focus on strong design, clear messaging, and sound setup within the agreed scope.
